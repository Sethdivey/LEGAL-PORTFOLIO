Data Breach Notification — Standard Operating Procedure

Ornamiint — Under the DPDP Act, 2023 & DPDP Rules, 2025

Entity: Ornamiint — online artificial jewellery business
Applicable Law: DPDP Act, 2023, Sec. 8(6); DPDP Rules, 2025, Rule 7
Incident Response Owners: Divey Seth and co-founder (sister) — jointly responsible

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

This SOP defines exactly what Ornamiint must do — and by when — if personal data it holds (Instagram DMs/handles, WhatsApp contacts, website order data, or Razorpay payment references) is lost, accessed without authorization, or otherwise compromised. It exists so that, in a real incident, the response is a checklist to follow, not a decision to make under pressure.

2. DETECTION & INITIAL TRIAGE (Hour 0)

The moment either owner becomes aware of a possible breach, the 72-hour regulatory clock begins — starting at awareness, not at the point the full picture is understood.

  1. Immediately secure the affected account/platform (change password, enable two-factor authentication, log out other sessions).
  2. Identify which platform is affected: Instagram, WhatsApp Business, the website, or Razorpay.
  3. Identify what data was likely exposed and estimate how many customers are affected.
  4. Note the exact date and time the issue was first noticed — this is the official "awareness" timestamp.

3. ESCALATION & NOTIFICATION TIMELINE

TimingActionOwner
Hour 0 (detection)Both owners alerted; initial triage beginsWhoever detects it
As soon as possible, without delayAffected customers notified directly via WhatsApp/Instagram DM and/or email, depending on available contact detailsDivey + sister
Without delay (initial alert)Data Protection Board informed of the nature, extent, timing, and likely impact of the breachDivey + sister
Within 72 hours of awarenessDetailed follow-up report submitted to the Board — full facts, cause, mitigation taken, prevention measuresDivey + sister

Note: there is no minimum-size threshold — a breach affecting even one customer triggers the same notification duty as one affecting many. If the breach also qualifies as a cybersecurity incident, CERT-In's separate 6-hour reporting rule may apply in parallel.

4. WHAT THE CUSTOMER NOTIFICATION MUST SAY

  • A plain-language description of what happened
  • Exactly what data of theirs was involved
  • What Ornamiint has done so far to contain it
  • Practical protective steps the customer can take
  • A direct contact for follow-up questions

5. CONTAINMENT, REMEDIATION & POST-INCIDENT REVIEW

Stop the exposure at its source, determine root cause, and document every step and timestamp — this record is what the 72-hour Board report is built from. Within one week of resolution, both owners jointly review whether the DPIA's Risk Register and Mitigation Plan, and this SOP itself, need updating based on what was actually learned.
