DPDP Act, 2023 — Data Privacy & Compliance Case Study

Paschim Vihar, Delhi-110063 | +91 7982819264 | sethdivey1@gmail.com | LinkedIn

This portfolio applies the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025 to Ornamiint — a real online artificial jewellery business I co-founded and operated with my sister across Instagram, WhatsApp Business, and a website built on Amazon Smart Biz, with Razorpay for payments and India Post for delivery. Rather than a hypothetical exercise, this is a genuine compliance audit of a business whose actual data practices I had first-hand knowledge of — its real gaps, and real, implementable fixes for each.

Exhibit 1 · Risk Assessment
Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)
Full data-flow mapping, legal basis and necessity review, a rated risk register, Data Principal rights impact, cross-border check, and a mitigation action plan — applied to Ornamiint's real operations.
 Exhibit 2 · Incident Response
Breach Notification Standard Operating Procedure
A real, usable incident-response playbook built around the DPDP Rules' 72-hour Board reporting requirement and "without delay" customer notification duty.
 Exhibit 3 · Consent Design
Consent Management Framework
A designed fix for the consent gaps the DPIA identified — real checkout language, a conversational-consent flow for WhatsApp/Instagram orders, and a withdrawal mechanism.

All three exhibits are connected — the Consent Framework and Breach SOP were built specifically to remediate gaps identified in the DPIA, mirroring how a real compliance function operates: assess, then design, then respond.
