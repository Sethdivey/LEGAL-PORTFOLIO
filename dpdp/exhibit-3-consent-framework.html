Consent Management Framework

Ornamiint — Designed under the DPDP Act, 2023, Sec. 4–6

Entity: Ornamiint — online artificial jewellery business
Purpose: A real, implementable consent capture and record-keeping system, addressing the gaps identified in Ornamiint's DPIA (Part B)

1. THE FIVE-PART CONSENT TEST APPLIED

PrincipleHow Ornamiint Implements It
FreeCheckout proceeds regardless of any optional marketing consent toggle — only core order-fulfilment consent is mandatory
SpecificSeparate, itemized lines for (a) order fulfilment and (b) optional future contact — never bundled
InformedPlain-language notice text — no legal jargon, no buried terms
Unconditional / UnambiguousAll consent checkboxes default to UNCHECKED — customer must actively tick
WithdrawableA single WhatsApp/Instagram message is treated as a valid, immediate withdrawal request

2. CONSENT CAPTURE — WEBSITE CHECKOUT

Two separate, unticked checkboxes at the final "Confirm Order" step:

[ ] I agree that my name, address, and order details will be used to process and deliver this order (required to proceed)
[ ] I'd also like to be notified about new collections and offers in the future (optional)

3. CONSENT CAPTURE — INSTAGRAM / WHATSAPP ORDERS

A standard message sent once per new customer, before finalizing their first order:

"Hi! Just so you know — we'll use your address only to deliver this order, and your number to send order updates. We won't message you for anything else unless you tell us you're okay with that. Reply YES to confirm you'd like to proceed."

This single message satisfies Notice + Consent for first-time customers in a conversational channel, and creates a clear, screenshot-able record of consent given.

4. WITHDRAWAL MECHANISM

ChannelHow a customer withdrawsOrnamiint's response
WhatsApp / InstagramAny message indicating they want to stop being contacted or want their data deletedRemove the "marketing OK" tag; if deletion requested, follow the DPIA's access/erasure process
WebsiteNo account system currently exists, so withdrawal is via a direct message using site contact detailsManually update the consent log entry for that customer

5. SPECIAL CASE — MINORS

If Ornamiint identifies that an order is being placed by someone under 18, the standard consent flow does not apply — verifiable parental consent is required per DPDP Sec. 9, and no promotional/marketing consent may be captured for that customer under any circumstance.
