PART D — DATA PRINCIPAL RIGHTS IMPACT

Scenario tested: a past customer requests "what data do you have on me, and I want it deleted."

Ornamiint would currently struggle to fulfil this cleanly — it could delete the Instagram thread and the WhatsApp label individually, but has no reliable way to verify nothing was missed, since the same customer's data was never cross-referenced across platforms. Recommendation: a minimum-viable internal customer log recording which platforms hold each customer's data.

PART E — CROSS-BORDER TRANSFER ASSESSMENT

Direct transfers: Razorpay (Indian, RBI-regulated) and India Post (domestic courier) — no direct cross-border transfer occurs through Ornamiint's own choices. Indirect consideration: Instagram and WhatsApp are operated by Meta (US-headquartered), whose global infrastructure may process data outside India as standard platform operation — worth reviewing as a Processor relationship if the business scales.
