Exhibit D — Privacy Policy Compliance Review
Context: Independent review of a live, publicly published privacy policy, conducted as a legal-drafting practice exercise. Tulua is a real Shark Tank India-featured D2C spice brand; the privacy policy reviewed is the version live on their website at the time of this review. Five representative issues are identified below, with the original published clause, the compliance concern, and a proposed correction for each.
Source reviewed: tulua.shop/policies/privacy-policy

PRIVACY POLICY COMPLIANCE REVIEW

Tulua (tulua.shop) — Independent Review Exercise

ISSUE 1 — UNREMOVED TEMPLATE DRAFTING NOTESFLAGGED
Original Published Clause

"[NOTE TO MERCHANT: This is a template Privacy Policy designed to cover your collection, use, and disclosure of personal information... Please also remember to delete all 'Note to Merchant' drafting notes prior to publishing.]" — published live on the site's public-facing privacy policy page.

Compliance Issue Identified

This is Shopify's standard privacy policy template, and the internal drafting instructions meant only for the merchant were never removed before publishing. The irony is direct: the note explicitly instructs the merchant to delete such notes before publishing, and that instruction itself was left in. This suggests the policy was never actually reviewed or customized to reflect Tulua's real data practices, which undermines its legal reliability as an accurate disclosure to users.

Proposed Correction

Remove all bracketed merchant instructions and drafting notes. Replace generic template language with clauses that accurately describe Tulua's actual data collection, storage, and sharing practices, reviewed and confirmed by the business itself, not left as unedited boilerplate.

ISSUE 2 — UNFILLED PLACEHOLDER FIELDSFLAGGED
Original Published Clause

"Last updated: [Date]" and "please call [TOLL FREE TELEPHONE NUMBER IF YOU HAVE A PHYSICAL RETAIL LOCATION]"

Compliance Issue Identified

Multiple placeholder fields remain unfilled in the published policy. The missing "Last updated" date is a genuine compliance gap: users and regulators have no way to know when the policy took effect, whether it reflects current practices, or whether it has been reviewed recently, which most data protection frameworks expect a privacy notice to state clearly.

Proposed Correction

Insert the actual last-revision date (e.g., "Last updated: [specific date]"), and either provide a real toll-free number or remove the placeholder and bracketed conditional text entirely if no such number exists.

ISSUE 3 — AMBIGUOUS DATA SALE/SHARING DISCLOSUREFLAGGED
Original Published Clause

"[NOTE TO MERCHANT- INSERT THE FOLLOWING PARAGRAPH AND CHART IF USING SHOPIFY'S AD SERVICES...]" followed immediately by a live table stating personal information "has 'sold' and 'shared'... with Business and marketing partners" for advertising purposes.

Compliance Issue Identified

The conditional merchant instruction ("insert IF using ad services") was never resolved, yet the substantive disclosure table beneath it was left in and reads as an active admission that Tulua sells or shares customer data with marketing partners. A user reading this cannot tell whether this is an accurate disclosure of real practice or an unintentional leftover from the template, a material ambiguity on exactly the kind of clause users and regulators scrutinize most closely.

Proposed Correction

Confirm definitively whether Tulua does or does not sell/share personal information with advertising partners, then either retain the table (with the conditional note removed) as an accurate, confirmed disclosure, or delete the entire section if it does not reflect actual practice.

ISSUE 4 — NO INDIAN DATA PROTECTION LAW COMPLIANCEFLAGGED
Original Published Clause

Policy is drafted entirely around US (CCPA-style "sell"/"share" language, "Global Privacy Control") and EU (GDPR "data controller", Standard Contractual Clauses) frameworks, with no reference to Indian law, despite Tulua's registered address being in Mumbai, Maharashtra.

Compliance Issue Identified

Tulua is an Indian entity serving primarily Indian customers, and is subject to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011. Critically, Indian law requires a named Grievance Officer to be published in the privacy policy, along with defined complaint-resolution timelines. The policy has only a generic contact email/phone with no Grievance Officer designated anywhere, which is a specific statutory omission, not just a stylistic gap.

Proposed Correction

Add a dedicated section naming a Grievance Officer (name, designation, email, postal address) as required under the IT Rules, 2011 and DPDPA, 2023, along with a stated response timeline (e.g., acknowledgment within 24 hours, resolution within 15-30 days, consistent with Indian regulatory expectations), in addition to (not instead of) the existing US/EU provisions if Tulua also serves those markets.

ISSUE 5 — CONTRADICTORY CHILDREN'S DATA CLAUSEFLAGGED
Original Published Clause

"The Services are not intended to be used by children, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information about children." Immediately followed by: "...we do not have actual knowledge that we 'share' or 'sell'... personal information of individuals under 16 years of age."

Compliance Issue Identified

The first sentence states a blanket policy against any collection of children's data (undefined age threshold). The second sentence, copied from CCPA's specific "under 16" sale/share framework, implies a more nuanced position limited to a specific US statutory age group and a specific set of activities (sale/sharing). The two statements are not reconciled and leave the actual applicable age threshold and scope of restriction unclear to an ordinary reader.

Proposed Correction

Define a single, consistent age threshold appropriate to the applicable jurisdiction (India's DPDPA defines a "child" as under 18, requiring verifiable parental consent for processing), and remove the CCPA-specific "sale/sharing under 16" language unless Tulua specifically operates in a US jurisdiction where that framework applies.
