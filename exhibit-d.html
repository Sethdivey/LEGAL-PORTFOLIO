Privacy Policy Compliance Review

Exhibit D · Regulatory Review — Tulua (tulua.shop)

Context: Independent review of a live, publicly published privacy policy, conducted as a legal-drafting practice exercise. Tulua is a real Shark Tank India-featured D2C spice brand.
Source reviewed: tulua.shop/policies/privacy-policy

ISSUE 1 — UNREMOVED TEMPLATE DRAFTING NOTES

Original published clause: "[NOTE TO MERCHANT: This is a template Privacy Policy... Please also remember to delete all 'Note to Merchant' drafting notes prior to publishing.]" — published live on the site's public-facing page.

Compliance issue identified: This is Shopify's standard privacy policy template, and the internal drafting instructions meant only for the merchant were never removed before publishing — suggesting the policy was never actually reviewed or customized to reflect Tulua's real data practices.

Proposed correction: Remove all bracketed merchant instructions and drafting notes. Replace generic template language with clauses that accurately describe Tulua's actual data collection, storage, and sharing practices.

ISSUE 2 — UNFILLED PLACEHOLDER FIELDS

Original published clause: "Last updated: [Date]" and "please call [TOLL FREE TELEPHONE NUMBER IF YOU HAVE A PHYSICAL RETAIL LOCATION]"

Compliance issue identified: The missing "Last updated" date is a genuine compliance gap — users and regulators have no way to know when the policy took effect or whether it reflects current practices.

Proposed correction: Insert the actual last-revision date, and either provide a real toll-free number or remove the placeholder and bracketed conditional text entirely.

ISSUE 3 — AMBIGUOUS DATA SALE/SHARING DISCLOSURE

Original published clause: "[NOTE TO MERCHANT — INSERT THE FOLLOWING PARAGRAPH AND CHART IF USING SHOPIFY'S AD SERVICES...]" followed immediately by a live table stating personal information "has 'sold' and 'shared'... with Business and marketing partners."

Compliance issue identified: The conditional merchant instruction was never resolved, yet the substantive disclosure table beneath it was left in and reads as an active admission that Tulua sells or shares customer data — a material ambiguity on exactly the kind of clause users and regulators scrutinize most closely.

Proposed correction: Confirm definitively whether Tulua does or does not sell/share personal information with advertising partners, then either retain the table (with the conditional note removed) as an accurate, confirmed disclosure, or delete the entire section.

ISSUE 4 — NO INDIAN DATA PROTECTION LAW COMPLIANCE

Original published clause: Policy is drafted entirely around US (CCPA-style) and EU (GDPR) frameworks, with no reference to Indian law, despite Tulua's registered address being in Mumbai, Maharashtra.

Compliance issue identified: Tulua is subject to India's DPDPA, 2023 and the IT Rules, 2011, which require a named Grievance Officer to be published in the privacy policy along with defined complaint-resolution timelines. The policy has only a generic contact email/phone with no Grievance Officer designated anywhere — a specific statutory omission, not just a stylistic gap.

Proposed correction: Add a dedicated section naming a Grievance Officer (name, designation, email, postal address) as required under the IT Rules, 2011 and DPDPA, 2023, along with a stated response timeline, in addition to the existing US/EU provisions.

ISSUE 5 — CONTRADICTORY CHILDREN'S DATA CLAUSE

Original published clause: "The Services are not intended to be used by children, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information about children." Immediately followed by CCPA-specific "under 16" sale/share language.

Compliance issue identified: The two statements are not reconciled and leave the actual applicable age threshold and scope of restriction unclear to an ordinary reader.

Proposed correction: Define a single, consistent age threshold appropriate to the applicable jurisdiction (India's DPDPA defines a "child" as under 18, requiring verifiable parental consent), and remove the CCPA-specific language unless Tulua specifically operates in a US jurisdiction where that framework applies.
