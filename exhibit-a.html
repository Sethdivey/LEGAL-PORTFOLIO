This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on this 28th day of July, 2026 ("Effective Date").

BY AND BETWEEN

Ornamiint Jewels Private Limited, a company registered under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 24C, BG-3, Paschim Vihar, Delhi-110063 (hereinafter referred to as "Party A", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include its successors and permitted assigns).

AND

Hrehar Consultants LLP, a limited liability partnership registered under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008, having its registered office at 229 Avtar Enclave, Paschim Vihar, Delhi-110063 (hereinafter referred to as "Party B", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include its successors and permitted assigns).

Party A and Party B are hereinafter collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Party A is engaged in the business related to artificial jewellery and possesses certain confidential technical and business information related to artificial jewellery;

WHEREAS, Party B is engaged in the business related to consultancy and has expressed interest in exploring a potential collaboration with Party A relating to retail operations expansion (the "Purpose");

WHEREAS, in order to enable the Parties to evaluate and pursue the Purpose, it may be required by either Party to disclose to the other some confidential and proprietary information; and

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to protect the confidential information in the manner set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means any and all information, whether oral, written, electronic, or in any other form, disclosed by either Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party") in accordance with the Purpose, including but not limited to: business plans, customer and vendor details, prices, profits and margins, technical information, per-unit cost structure of jewellery articles, sourcing and supplier details for artificial jewellery, and any other information that is designated as Confidential Information at the time of disclosure or that a reasonable person would consider confidential based on the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

2. EXCLUSIONS

Confidential Information shall not include information that:

(a) was already known to the Receiving Party prior to the disclosure of Confidential Information to the Receiving Party by the Disclosing Party, without any obligation of confidentiality;
(b) is or becomes available to the general public through no fault or breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;
(c) is independently discovered by the Receiving Party without reference to or use of the Confidential Information disclosed by the Disclosing Party;
(d) is or was rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without any obligation of confidentiality owed to the Disclosing Party; or
(e) is required to be disclosed as per applicable law, regulations, or a valid order of a court or government authority, provided that the Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prior written notice of such requirement, to the extent legally permitted.

3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

The Receiving Party agrees that it shall:

(a) use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating and pursuing the Purpose, and for no other purpose whatsoever;
(b) not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent by the Disclosing Party, except to its employees, directors, or any other professional advisors or company professionals for whom it may be necessary to disclose such Confidential Information for the Purpose, and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than those contained herein;
(c) protect the Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses to protect its own information of a similar nature, and in any event not less than reasonable care;
(d) not copy, publish, pass on to competitors, or reproduce any Confidential Information, except as reasonably necessary for the Purpose;
(e) promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing upon becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure of the Confidential Information.

4. TERM

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in force for a period of 6 months, unless terminated earlier by either Party upon 30 days' prior written notice to the other Party. Notwithstanding the termination or expiry of this Agreement, the confidentiality obligations set out in Clause 3 shall remain in force for a further period of 5 years from the date of such termination or expiry.

5. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Upon written request of the Disclosing Party, or on termination or expiry of this Agreement, whichever is earlier, the Receiving Party shall promptly, and in any event within 15 days of such request or termination, return or destroy all the Confidential Information in its possession or control, including all copies, extracts, and summaries thereof, in whatever form held, and shall certify such return or destruction in writing, signed by an authorised person of the Receiving Party.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Receiving Party may retain a copy of the Confidential Information solely to the extent required to comply with applicable law or its bona fide document retention policies, provided such retained copy remains subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement.

6. REMEDIES

The Receiving Party acknowledges that it will be liable for any harm or damage caused to the Disclosing Party due to any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement, for which monetary compensation alone may not be an adequate remedy. Accordingly, the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, or any other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction, in addition to any other rights and remedies available to it under this Agreement or applicable law, without the necessity of posting a bond or proving actual damages.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. The courts at Delhi shall have exclusive jurisdiction to entertain any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

8. MISCELLANEOUS

8.1 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties with respect to the subject matter hereof. All prior discussions, agreements, or negotiations, whether oral or written, relating to such subject matter will be superseded by this Agreement.

8.2 Amendment. This Agreement may only be amended by a written instrument signed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

8.3 Assignment. Rights and obligations arising under this Agreement, including this Agreement itself, shall not be transferred or assigned to any third party by either Party without the prior written consent of the other Party.

8.4 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

8.5 Waiver. No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver of such right, nor shall any single or partial exercise preclude any other or further exercise of such right.

8.6 Notices. The Parties shall deliver any notice required under this Agreement in writing, by registered post or email, to the addresses of the Parties set out above.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date first written above.
