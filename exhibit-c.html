SUIT FOR EJECTMENT AND RECOVERY OF MESNE PROFITS

The abovenamed Plaintiff most respectfully submits as under:—

1. That the Plaintiff let out his property situate at House No. 17, Block B, Lajpat Nagar-II, New Delhi-110024 to the Defendant for a fixed period of 11 months from 1st June, 2024 to 30th April, 2025, at a monthly rent of Rs. 35,000 (Rupees Thirty-Five Thousand only), and the whole rent for the period was paid in advance on 1st June, 2024.

2. That the term aforesaid has expired but the Defendant has not vacated the property abovementioned.

3. That the cause of action arose on 1st May, 2025, being the day immediately following the expiry of the term of tenancy on 30th April, 2025, and this Court has jurisdiction to decide the matter.

4. That the suit is valued at Rs. 4,20,000 (Rupees Four Lakh Twenty Thousand only), being twelve times the monthly rent, for the purpose of jurisdiction, and at Rs. 4,20,000 (Rupees Four Lakh Twenty Thousand only) for the purpose of court fee.

RELIEFS CLAIMED:

That the Plaintiff claims:

(1) Eviction of the Defendant from the property abovementioned and delivery of possession of the said property to the Plaintiff.

(2) Mesne profits from 1st May, 2025 till the date of filing of this suit, and pendente lite, and until possession is delivered to the Plaintiff.

Plaintiff
Through Advocate
